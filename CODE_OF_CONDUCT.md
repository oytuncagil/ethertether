# Code of conduct

EtherTether is a small project with one maintainer. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, agrees to these rules.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are not native English speakers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Keep the discussion about the work: the code, the docs, the behaviour of the app.
- Accept that the maintainer makes the final decision on what goes into the project.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission. This includes data in logs and screenshots: remove serial numbers, addresses and anything personal before you post.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove posts, close issues, and block accounts that break these rules, with or without a warning. To report a problem privately, use the "Report content" function of GitHub on the post in question, or the private vulnerability report described in SECURITY.md if it is urgent.
